Bed Bahadur Gurung

A public servant and storyteller devoted to the people of Madi and the wider Gandaki Province.

From leading local government as Chairman of Madi Rural Municipality to representing constituents in the Gandaki Province Assembly, Bed Bahadur Gurung has built a career grounded in public service. Alongside his political work, he has shaped Nepali cinema as an actor, writer, and director — leading Abhiyan Film Production and using storytelling as a tool for social awareness and community upliftment.
